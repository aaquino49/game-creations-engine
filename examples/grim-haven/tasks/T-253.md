## Rules

- Follow ART_GUIDE v2 to the letter: palette (quantize every pixel to `game/assets/palette.json`), 1 px outline, light from top left, 3/4 top-down, units face right, 32x32 frames, the sheet JSON contract (idle 2-4 frames, attack/hurt/death 3-6, attack <= 0.5 s, death <= 0.6 s, death ends on a corpse pose).
- Prefer the recommended packs from `project/art/PACK_RESEARCH.md`; edit/recolour to the palette. Fill gaps with ChatGPT image generation using the ART_GUIDE v2 pipeline (generate large, downscale to the grid, quantize, clean by hand).
- Tiles must read at a glance at 64 px: floor quiet and dark; walls clearly solid; **half cover visibly lower than full cover** (e.g. a low crate/tyre stack vs a car wreck/scrap wall); hazard obviously dangerous (fire/oil); exit obvious (gate/hatch with a `gold` or `ember` cue). Floors tile seamlessly in any 3x3 mix.
- Bruiser and Thug silhouettes must differ at 32 px (hero bone + class accent vs raider rust/red spiky scrap), and both read against the floor.
- Portrait: the ART_GUIDE v2 prompt template, painted graphic-novel, bold ink, flat colour with painted shading, simple background; must match the Bruiser sprite's outfit and colours.
