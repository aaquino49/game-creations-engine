## The three fixes (R-4)

1. **Faction colour.** Heroes read bone/cream with teal/steel-blue accents (the Bruiser keeps its class accent, per §2's class table); Raiders read rust/orange/red. At a glance on the board the Bruiser and the Thug must look like **two different teams**. Brighter unit values than the T-253 sprites: the lightest body ramp on each unit is at least 2 palette steps lighter than the floor's lightest colour.
2. **Units fill the cell and stand off the floor.** Bodies about **26-30 px tall** inside the 32x32 frame (T-253's are ~16-18 px). Feet on y 29-30. Keep the 1 px outline, but add a lighter rim or outline on the floor side as §2/§3 now specify, and a **contact shadow** (an ellipse of the shadow colour about 16x4 px under the feet, painted into every frame, including the death pose).
3. **Lighter, calmer floor.** Floor 1-3 a value step lighter than T-253's, noise cut by about half; **no black blobs** (they read as pits). Scatter is decorative and lower-contrast than cover. Value order on the board, brightest first: units, then walls and cover, then the floor. Hazard still reads as burning, exit keeps its gold/ember cue (§10).

Keep: 32 px detail level, 1 px outline, top-left light, units face right, key-pose sets (idle 2-4, attack/hurt/death 3-6 frames; attack <= 0.5 s; death <= 0.6 s ending on a corpse pose). The painted Bruiser portrait is approved: do not touch it.

#### What to change (numbers come from ART_GUIDE v2.1; T-253 values in brackets, measured by `project/art/check_v21_readability.py`, see `project/runs/T-254/a1/readability_before.jsonl`)

**Bruiser (hero):**
- Body 28-30 px tall without the raised maul, shoulders 16-20 px wide, limbs ≥ 2 px + outline, idle-0 opaque ≥ 450 [461 opaque but 46% of it `void`, so the visible body was thin].
- Colours: coat/trousers `bone`/`sand` with `wood`/`skin_dark` shade = **≥ 35% faction main** [10%]; a **teal** neckerchief or shoulder patch (4-10 px, `moss`/`teal`/`glass`) as the hero marker; `rust` only on the forearm wrap and maul grip (≤ 15%); maul head steel in `legion`/`legion_light` or `stone`/`dust`.
- **`void` ≤ 20%** [45.8%]: outline only on the outer silhouette; interior lines in `wood`, `skin_dark`, `stone`. **Light pixels (L ≥ 0.58) ≥ 25%** [21.3%] with a 1 px `bone` rim on head/shoulder/maul top-left edges.

**Raider Thug (enemy):**
- Body 26-28 px (a lean brawler, smaller than the Bruiser but not a stick figure), torso ≥ 12 px wide, spiky shoulder plate, mohawk.
- Colours: armour/skin wraps `rust`/`rust_light` with `rust_dark` shade and `ember` highlights = **≥ 35% faction main** [14.2%]; **`scarlet` war paint and rag** as the marker; no `bone` cloth (that is the hero colour).
- **`void` ≤ 20%** [51%], **light ≥ 25%** [15.2%], 1 px `ember` rim top-left.

**Both units:** keep the frame counts and fps (idle 4@4, attack 4@12, hurt 3@12, death 5@10) and anchor [16,16]; static = idle frame 0. Add the **`soot` contact shadow** on rows 30-31 (12-18 px wide, centred on x = 16) in every frame, widening under the corpse. Idle adds one **secondary 1 px motion** (head, weapon tip or rag) out of phase with the breathing. The two units must read as different teams from colour alone in a 720p capture: bone+teal vs orange+red.

**Scrapfields kit:**
- Floors 1-3: base **`earth`** (~85%), `ash` variation, small scatter in `stone`/`wood` (each piece ≤ 3x3 px, ≤ 8% total), 1-3 single specks of `dust`/`sand`/`rust`. **No `void`, no `soot` areas, no dark blobs** [T-253: 92% `soot`, floor 3 had 15% `void` rock blobs that read as pits; largest darker-than-ash blob 940 px]. Target mean L 0.38-0.43 [0.24]. Tile seamlessly in a 3x3 mosaic with no visible repeat spots.
- Walls and cover: same shapes as T-253 are fine (crushed car, scrap wall, tyre stack, crate, car wreck, locker/plate barricade), redrawn so their **lit faces use L ≥ 0.55** (`dust`, `sand`, `rust_light`, `legion`, `bone` edges), `void` outline toward the floor, background pixels = the new floor (`earth`), not `soot`. Half cover ≤ 14 px tall, full cover ≥ 24 px. The tyre stack (half cover 1) must read as tyres on the lighter floor (`ash`/`stone` rubber with a `dust` rim), not as a black hole.
- Hazard (burning oil) and exit (chain-link gate, `gold` cue): redraw on the `earth` base; the oil pool may use `rust_dark`/`ash` but no `void`.
